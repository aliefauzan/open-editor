# Listen check

Mix: `mix.wav`, 30 fps, 2 cues.

These timecodes come from measurements. The numbers in `qa.json` do not say the sound is
good; only a person can judge that. Listen to each moment and mark it ok or not.

1. `00:06.200` (hero cue): cue 1 (hero): one clean hit that lands with the picture
2. `00:14.012` (loud SFX): peak -1.2 dBFS: harshness, or a hit that covers the voice
