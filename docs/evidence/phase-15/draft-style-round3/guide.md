# Attention Compound

A draft extracted from a reference video. It re-skins vox; the sections below describe the reference and add to the vox guide.

## Reference look (extracted)

- Summary: The video's visual style is a dynamic, narrative-driven montage that juxtaposes high-fidelity live-action footage of a stadium performance with highly stylized, surreal, and cinematic digital composites. The aesthetic relies on a 'text-as-voiceover' technique where captions are presented as raw, lowercase fragments (e.g., 'this wasn't just a', 'performing'), creating a personal, confessional tone against the epic backdrop. Recurring layouts include extreme close-ups of eyes acting as portals, full-frame title cards that reveal key story beats, and vertical panels with 'halftone polaroid' style portraits that introduce characters or concepts.
- Motion: entrance fade, frame hold 3, camera moves: zoom in slow push, pan track subject, static steady shot.
- Motion notes: Transitions are often abrupt cuts that jump between reality and the 'digital world'. Within scenes, there is a steady, deliberate push-in on subjects and objects (eyes, mic, house). Camera movements in live-action shots are handheld or gimbal-stabilized for an intimate feel.
- Texture: no paper, grain 0.2.
- Palette (largest first): #151519 27 %, #473428 18 %, #745745 15 %, #C7B59F 10 %, #B97E54 10 %, #E2E8E8 8 %, #7C7E9F 6 %, #26436A 6 %.
