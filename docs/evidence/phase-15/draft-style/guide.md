# Attention Compound

A draft extracted from a reference video. It re-skins vox; the sections below describe the reference and add to the vox guide.

## Reference look (extracted)

- Summary: A documentary-style montage about a Super Bowl halftime show performance that doubles as an elaborate story. The visual style is highly stylized, using macro shots of eyes and close-ups of feet to create a surreal, dreamlike quality. The text overlay acts as a narrator's voice, guiding the viewer through a narrative that blends reality with fantasy (e.g., 'this wasn't just a performance' vs. 'it was a story'). The color palette is warm and textured, emphasizing paper grain, dust, and soft lighting.
- Motion: entrance fade, frame hold 2, camera moves: macro-zoom, pan, tumble.
- Motion notes: Macro shots of eyes often start with a soft macro zoom. The video cuts between wide stadium shots and intimate close-ups (feet on grass, hands in pockets). Transitions are often fast and jarring.
- Texture: paper, grain 0.02, film look.
- Palette (largest first): #151517 26 %, #493428 17 %, #745746 15 %, #C3B4A1 11 %, #B97F56 10 %, #E2E8E8 8 %, #20395A 7 %, #6F749D 6 %.

## Pacing (extracted)

- A speaker is on screen 67 % of the time.
